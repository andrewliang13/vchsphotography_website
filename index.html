@vchs_photography

A place to practice seeing.

VCHS Photography brings students together to learn technique, share critique, and document school life with care.

Phones, borrowed cameras, and personal gear are all welcome. No previous experience is required.

This year we are building curriculum, community partnerships, fundraisers, and this website, so student work has somewhere to live. The club also collaborates with VCS Marketing.
