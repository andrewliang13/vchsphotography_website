Donate & Sponsor

Support student gear access and community shoots.

The club is building a shared equipment library so students can participate without purchasing every piece of gear themselves. Support also helps the club provide photography services to community organizations.

Why support us

Equipment access creates opportunity.

Roughly 30 active members photograph campus life and community projects. Shared cameras, lenses, and lighting make those assignments possible for students who do not own professional gear.

Current resources

What members already use.

Personal cameras and lenses contributed or used by members, a tripod, a backdrop, and a reflective umbrella. Lightroom is expected to be provided by the school; confirmation is pending. These are current resources, not claims of club-owned inventory unless ownership is confirmed.

Sponsors

Partners who make shared gear possible.

Suggested levels

Contribution ranges, not minimums.

Sponsors may offer a different amount or an in-kind contribution.

Equipment Supporter

$50–200 · Helps fund smaller essentials such as memory cards, batteries, storage, filters, and accessories.

Project Sponsor

$250–500 · Helps support community projects, portrait lighting, printing, and approved transportation expenses.

Camera Sponsor

$500–1,000 · Helps fund shared camera bodies or lenses, giving more students access to quality equipment.

In-kind Contributions

Functional used cameras, lenses, lighting, printing, storage services, mentorship, and other useful resources.

Recognition

Subject to school approval.

Potential benefits: recognition on this sponsor page, a thank-you letter, acknowledgment in an approved project recap or exhibition, and an invitation to a future student photography exhibition if one is organized. We do not guarantee advertising reach or exclusive promotion. See the exhibitions page for status — none are scheduled yet.

Important

School-approved donations only.

The club is not represented as an independent 501(c)(3). Do not assume contributions are tax-deductible through the club. A future independent nonprofit is being explored, but it is not current. There is no online payment flow on this site.
